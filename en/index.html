Process

From first call to operations,
in five clear steps

Every step comes with shared deliverables and timelines. You always know where we are and what comes next.

  1. STEP 01

    Discovery

    We review your goals and current systems, then define the scope.

    1 week

  2. STEP 02

    Planning

    Information architecture, wireframes, payment flows and data models.

    1–2 weeks

  3. STEP 03

    Design

    Brand-fit UI and design system, validated with prototypes.

    2–3 weeks

  4. STEP 04

    Build & Integrate

    Front/back-end, PG, logistics and third-party APIs, then QA.

    3–6 weeks

  5. STEP 05

    Launch & Run

    Stabilization, then maintenance, monitoring and growth.

    Ongoing
